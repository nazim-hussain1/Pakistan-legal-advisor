Pakistan Legal Advisor

An intelligent legal assistant developed to provide access to verified Pakistani constitutional and statutory law, bridging language barriers and technical gaps for all citizens.

rocket_launch
Mission of this Project
To make legal knowledge universally accessible across Pakistan by delivering precise, AI-driven guidance in English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu. This system bridges the gap between complex legal statutes and everyday citizens, students, and legal professionals through an advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework grounded entirely in verified Pakistani law.
school
Academic Inception
Developed as a Final Year Project at Quaid-e-Awam University of Engineering, Science & Technology, Nawabshah. Built with a commitment to public service equipping students, citizens, and legal practitioners with reliable, language-inclusive access to the Constitution of Pakistan and key legislative provisions, reducing barriers to justice through intelligent technology.
person
Nazim Hussain
Project Creator

Developed by Nazim Hussain under the academic supervision of Zeeshan Rasool at QUEST Nawabshah, this platform was created to democratize legal accessibility in Pakistan by bridging linguistic and technical gaps for everyday citizens. Built as part of a BS in Artificial Intelligence thesis, the system utilizes a hybrid neural search architecture integrating sparse BM25 representations, Cross-Encoder reranking, and multilingual generative alignment to deliver highly accurate, localized legal retrieval.
